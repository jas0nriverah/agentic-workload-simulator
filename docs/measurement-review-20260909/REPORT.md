## Scope and stopping rule

PDF-required evidence is the measurement chain above. Evidence needed for scientific validity includes attempt/request/host/clock identity, capture loss and censoring, actual workload state, concurrency attribution and preservation of raw artifacts. A concrete omission in either category may delay production because it can force reacquisition.

Verbose monitoring, redundant snapshots and human-readable copies are debugging conveniences. Blanket kernel/instruction tracing, arbitrary hardware counters, new models or expanded campaigns are speculative unless a concrete required quantity cannot otherwise be recovered. They do not delay production.

Measurement completeness does not certify final D9 accuracy, literal scoreboard reproduction where reference data are absent, current GPU health, or storage capacity. After independent objections close and completeness passes, stop this audit and resume the predeclared configuration process, followed by selection, final freeze/preflight and production.
